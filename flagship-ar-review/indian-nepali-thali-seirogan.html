Nepali Thali

Indian section · a composed sharing tray

Judge this one differently. It is here because a thali shows quantity — how many components, how far they spread, how much table it takes. Look at that first, then judge the surface.

Place it, sit back and read the whole tray. Then go close on the dal bowl and the rice. Then look along the table at the tray edge, and from behind.
