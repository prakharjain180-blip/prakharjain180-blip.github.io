Thirteen files came back from the manual queue. Two earned a place here. None of the thirteen was a credible new Indian dish: the one correct Indian identity, dal tadka with naan, sits in checkered deli paper.

The samuelomo captures carry 2048×2048 textures on 10–22k triangles; the burger carries 1024 on 60k. Every USDZ here ships a 1024 texture — the exporter's default. A 2048 build was made and compared: 3.5× the download for a gain the captures' own focus does not support.

Sizes on the six samuelomo dishes were corrected on 27 September to each capture's own units, read as millimetres. The previous build used chosen widths that ran from 18% small to 13% large.
